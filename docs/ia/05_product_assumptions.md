# Product assumptions (Phase 1)

Until a fuller product spec exists, the interface assumes:

- Market: Malaysia, currency RM
- Supply: creators who operate one or more **virtual KOLs** (avatars)
- Demand: F&B businesses (restaurants, groups) who buy campaigns
- Ops: managers QC content; admins oversee the platform
- **AMF (Avatar Market-Fit)** and KOL–campaign match are **predicted** scores with inspectable factors
- Campaign lifecycle: brief → match → accept → produce → QC → manager review → business approval → publish → payout
- Business workspace is created at registration; creator studio is created at registration
- Guest landing prompt is an ephemeral workspace that promotes on signup

These assumptions are implementation defaults, not locked commercial terms.
