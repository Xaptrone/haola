# Product assumptions

Until a fuller product spec exists, the interface assumes:

- Market: Malaysia, currency RM. ICP: **broad SMEs** (retail, clinics, services, F&B as one vertical — not the product center)
- Supply: creators who operate one or more **virtual KOLs** (avatars)
- Demand: businesses who buy campaigns, Brand IP packs, and content packs
- Ops: managers QC content; admins oversee the platform and credit top-ups
- **AMF (Avatar Market-Fit)** and KOL–campaign match are **predicted** scores with inspectable factors
- Campaign lifecycle: brief → match → accept (credit hold) → produce → 3-step review → final approve (release) → publish
- Review pipeline: script + assets → rough video → edited video (one revision)
- Credits: append-only ledger, 1 credit = RM 1.00. Admin manual top-up. Escrow on accept.
- Business workspace is created at registration; creator studio is created at registration
- Guest landing prompt is an ephemeral workspace that promotes on signup
- Same business workspace, two seats: **owner** (spend) and **marketing** (submit briefs)

These assumptions are implementation defaults, not locked commercial terms.
