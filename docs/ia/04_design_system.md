# Design system

One language for landing, creator studio, business command, manager oversight.

## Intent

AI-native, young, intelligent, creative, minimal, premium, fast, trustworthy.

## 60 / 30 / 10

- 60% canvas: `#12110F`
- 30% surface: `#1E1C18`
- 10% accent: `#FF5C1A` ember — one CTA, active nav, score emphasis
- Ink `#F4F0EA` / muted `#9A948A`
- Line `rgba(244, 240, 234, 0.10)`
- Sage success, amber warning, rose only for reject / QC fail

## Type

Sora 400 / 500 / 600. Two or three sizes per view. Scores may use a tabular numeric face.

## Space and shape

4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48. Cards 12px, sheets 24px, chips pill. Tap 44px min, primary 56px. Motion ≤ 200ms.

## Components (single family)

Button, StatusChip, ProvenanceMark, ScoreRing, ActionCard, VerticalVideo, Empty / Loading / Error, BottomNav, CanvasBoard, CommandFeed.

One card elevation. No gradient skins. No decorative AI imagery.

## Video

9:16 stage. Full-bleed on mobile review. Desktop review may split player + decision rail.

## States

Empty (blank canvas or “nothing needs you”), loading skeletons that keep layout, errors inline next to the action.
